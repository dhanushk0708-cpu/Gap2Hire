# Gap2Hire AI Evaluation

## 1. Purpose

AI outputs shall be evaluated to ensure they are accurate, useful, consistent, and safe enough for their intended use.

## 2. Evaluation Areas

Gap2Hire will evaluate:

* Job description → capability extraction
* Resume → candidate information extraction
* Candidate evidence classification
* Gap and uncertainty identification
* Verification recommendations
* AI interview relevance
* RAG answer accuracy
* Personalized training recommendations

## 3. Evaluation Criteria

AI features should be evaluated for:

* Accuracy
* Relevance
* Consistency
* Structured output validity
* Hallucination rate
* Safety
* Traceability

## 4. Evaluation Method

The project will maintain representative test examples and expected results for important AI workflows.

AI outputs will be compared against these examples before major changes are accepted.

## 5. Human Review

Important AI-generated results shall support human review.

Human feedback may be used to improve prompts, evaluation datasets, and system behavior.

## 6. Evaluation Principle

> **AI features are not considered complete simply because they produce an output; their quality must be measured against defined expectations.**
